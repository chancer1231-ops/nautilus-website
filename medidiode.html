How It Works

Targeting Pigment, Cycle by Cycle

The laser targets the pigment in the hair itself. That energy travels down the shaft to the follicle and gradually shuts it down — but only for hairs that are actively growing at the moment of treatment.

Hair grows in cycles, and at any given moment only a portion of your hair is in that active phase. That's the entire reason laser hair reduction is a series rather than a single visit — each session catches a different portion of the cycle. Spacing your sessions consistently is what makes the series actually work.

It's also why we say reduction rather than removal. Results build progressively and meaningfully — but honest language matters more to us than a bigger promise.

What to expect

A series of sessions spaced several weeks apart, with visible thinning and shedding building over time. We'll walk you through pre- and post-care at your first visit — and we'll tell you honestly how many sessions your hair and skin are likely to need.
