What's Inside

The Seven Modalities

Each handpiece does something specific. An Advanced Facial is a sequence chosen from these — which is why two clients can book the same treatment and have genuinely different sessions.

Hydradermabrasion 01

Exfoliation, cleansing and extraction in one pass — lifting away dead skin and debris while infusing hydration back into the skin.

Aqua Peel Suction 02

Vacuum-assisted extraction that clears congestion from pores. The suction line self-cleans between clients.

Bipolar Radiofrequency 03

Six contact points deliver gentle, controlled heat into the deeper layers to encourage firmness. This is the warmth you may feel during a treatment.

Microcurrent 04

Low-level current that works the facial muscles for lift and tone. This is the light muscle sensation — noticeable, but it shouldn't be uncomfortable.

Oxygen Infusion 05

An internal oxygen concentrator delivers pure oxygen alongside serum to brighten and calm the skin.

Hot / Cold Wand 06

Warms to 42°C to soften and prepare the skin, then cools to 5°C to soothe and settle it afterward.

CO₂ Buffing Tip 07

A carbonic buffing step that refines surface texture and adds brightness.

What to expect

No downtime, no discomfort. Some modalities may include gentle warmth or light muscle sensation — all normal, all part of the process. You'll leave with a glow, not redness or irritation.

Where Most Journeys Begin

The Treatment That Tells Us the Most

An Advanced Facial is usually the first thing we book for a new client — not because it's the smallest commitment, but because it's the most informative.

Working across the whole face with seven different tools tells us how your skin behaves: where it's congested, where the barrier is compromised, how it responds to warmth and current. That's a far better foundation for a plan than a visual consultation alone.

It's also how we get to know each other before recommending anything more involved. In the Nautilus Evolve Method, this is Restore and Rebalance — the foundation everything else is built on.
