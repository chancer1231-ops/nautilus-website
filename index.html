The Devices Behind the Results

We chose American-made Medicreations technology and the FDA-cleared Jett Plasma Medical II because the tools matter as much as the hands using them.

Medicreations · Made in USA

MediDiode

A diode laser built for hair reduction in constant motion — a 12×12 mm spot delivered in a smooth glide rather than stamp-by-stamp, and safe across all six Fitzpatrick skin types.
