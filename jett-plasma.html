How It Works

Your Own Collagen, Prompted

Jett Plasma delivers controlled plasma energy at the skin's surface. That energy signals the fibroblasts underneath — the cells responsible for collagen and elastin — to begin producing new structural tissue.

Nothing is added and nothing is removed. The result is built by your own body, which is why it appears gradually over the weeks following a treatment rather than immediately afterward.

The word non-ablative is the important one. Ablative treatments work by removing the surface of the skin, which is what produces the crusting and peeling people associate with resurfacing. This device works without breaking the surface at all — so there's nothing to plan your week around.

Non-ablative — the surface of the skin stays intact

What to expect

Results build over the weeks that follow rather than appearing all at once — your body is doing the work on its own schedule. This treatment addresses fine lines and wrinkles; it isn't the right tool for pigment or lesions, and we'll always tell you honestly if something else would serve you better.

About the clearance. The Jett Plasma Medical II (manufactured by COMPEX, spol. s r.o.) received FDA 510(k) clearance K240311 and is indicated for non-ablative treatment of mild to moderate facial wrinkles and rhytids for skin phototypes I–IV. It is a prescription device. Because the clearance covers phototypes I–IV, we confirm your Fitzpatrick type at consultation and will recommend a different approach if this one isn't appropriate for your skin.
