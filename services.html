Skin Rejuvenation

Photo Facial / IPL

Intense Pulsed Light therapy is one of the most effective non-invasive treatments for skin tone correction and rejuvenation. Targeted pulses of light penetrate beneath the surface to address damage, discoloration, and the visible signs of aging.

Ideal for sun damage, age spots, rosacea, broken capillaries, and uneven pigmentation — IPL delivers a clearer, more luminous complexion with minimal disruption to your routine.

Reduces sun damage, age spots and hyperpigmentation
Targets rosacea, redness and broken capillaries
Stimulates collagen for firmer, more youthful skin
Minimal downtime — light sensitivity for 24–48 hours
Progressive improvement with each treatment
